ABOUT GHAR SATHI

Your Home Deserves Reliable Care.

Ghar Sathi is a home-services platform that connects customers with technicians for AC service, washing machine repair, refrigerator repair, RO service, electrician, and other home maintenance needs.

We understand that finding a suitable technician and coordinating service when a problem arises with any household item can be difficult.Ghar Sathi's goal is to simplify this process.

Our platform aims to make it easier for customers to find the right technician for their home service needs, while also providing technicians with more opportunities to connect with potential customers.

Our mission is to make home services more convenient and accessible for every household, while also helping technicians grow their business and reach more customers.
